# Econ 101 – Week 3

## Demand
How much people want to buy at each price.

When the price goes up, the quantity demanded goes down (law of demand).

## Law of demand
The rule that when the price goes up, the quantity demanded goes down.

## Supply
How much sellers offer at each price.

When the price goes up, the quantity supplied goes up.

## Equilibrium
The point where the supply and demand curves cross, with no shortage and no surplus.

The market "clears".

## Price elasticity of demand
How strongly the quantity demanded reacts to a price change.

- Elastic: big reaction (e.g. restaurant meals)
- Inelastic: small reaction (e.g. salt, insulin)

## Elastic demand
Demand where the quantity demanded reacts strongly to a price change.

Example: restaurant meals.

## Inelastic demand
Demand where the quantity demanded reacts only a little to a price change.

Examples: salt, insulin.

## Substitute goods
Goods that can replace each other.

Example: tea and coffee. When the price of coffee goes up, the demand for tea goes up.
