# Stars - The Night Sky

Notes from an evening with a star map. Every question callout is a card; other callouts stay notes.

> [!question] What is the brightest star in the night sky?
> Sirius, in the constellation Canis Major.

> [!question]- Which planet is called the Red Planet?
> Mars. Dust rich in iron oxide gives its surface the red colour.

> [!question]
> The Moon always shows Earth the same face.
> Why?
> > [!answer]-
> > It turns once on its axis in the time it takes to go around Earth, about 27 days.

> [!note] Tonight
> Clear sky after 22:00, the Moon sets early.

> [!faq] What is a shooting star?
> A small piece of rock burning up in the air high above us, called a meteor.

> [!question] Which star shows the way north?
> Polaris, the North Star, at the end of the handle of the Little Dipper.

> [!question] How long does sunlight take to reach Earth?
> About 8 minutes and 20 seconds.
