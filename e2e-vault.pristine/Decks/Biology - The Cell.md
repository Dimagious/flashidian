# Biology - The Cell

Parts of a eukaryotic cell. Every `##` heading is a card; the text under it is the answer.

## Nucleus

The nucleus is the control centre of the cell: it holds the DNA and decides which proteins get made.

> [!tip] Remember
> Red blood cells in mammals lose their nucleus as they mature.

## Mitochondrion

A mitochondrion turns glucose and oxygen into ATP, the energy the cell runs on. It has its own small loop of DNA.

## Ribosome

A ribosome reads messenger RNA and joins amino acids into a protein. Ribosomes float in the cytoplasm or sit on the rough endoplasmic reticulum.

## Cell membrane

The cell membrane is a thin double layer of lipids around the cell that lets some molecules in and keeps others out.

- Water passes freely.
- Ions need channels.

## Cytoplasm

The cytoplasm is the jelly that fills the cell between the membrane and the nucleus; the organelles float in it.

## Golgi apparatus

The Golgi apparatus packs proteins into small bubbles and ships them to where they are needed, inside or outside the cell.

## Lysosome

A lysosome is a bag of digestive enzymes that breaks down worn-out parts of the cell and anything it swallows.

## Chloroplast

A chloroplast captures sunlight and makes sugar from water and carbon dioxide. Only plant cells and algae have them.
