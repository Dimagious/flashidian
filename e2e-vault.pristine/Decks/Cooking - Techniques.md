# Cooking - Techniques

- Sauté - to sauté is to cook small pieces quickly in a little hot fat, moving them so they brown evenly.
- Braise - to braise is to brown meat first, then cook it slowly in a little liquid under a lid.
- Blanch - to blanch is to plunge vegetables into boiling water for a minute, then into ice water.
- Julienne - julienne means cutting vegetables into thin matchsticks of the same size.
- Deglaze - to deglaze is to pour wine or stock into a hot pan to lift the browned bits off the bottom.
- Emulsify - to emulsify is to whisk two liquids that do not mix, like oil and vinegar, into a smooth sauce.
- Fold - to fold is to mix gently with a spatula, lifting from the bottom, so the batter keeps its air.
- Proof - to proof is to let a yeast dough rest in a warm place until it rises.
