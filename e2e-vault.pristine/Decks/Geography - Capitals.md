# Geography - Capitals

| Country | Capital | Known for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| France | Paris | The Eiffel Tower, built for the 1889 World's Fair |
| Japan | Tokyo | The largest metropolitan area in the world |
| Brazil | Brasília | A capital built from scratch in the 1950s |
| Australia | Canberra | Chosen as a compromise between Sydney and Melbourne |
| Canada | Ottawa | The Rideau Canal, a skating rink in winter |
| Egypt | Cairo | The pyramids of Giza at the edge of the city |
| Kenya | Nairobi | A national park inside the city limits |
| Italy | Rome | The Colosseum and Vatican City |
| Mexico | Mexico City | Built on the lake bed of the Aztec capital |
| Turkey | Ankara | Not Istanbul, which is the largest city |
